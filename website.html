About me

A bit about me & how I work

I'm a freelance developer with a background in data science. I build practical AI-powered software for startups, small businesses, and AI teams — custom web apps, SaaS-style MVPs, workflow automation, annotation and review dashboards, browser-based AI tools, and Android or Flutter apps.

I keep things simple and honest. We agree on a clear scope first, I explain things in plain language, and I keep core delivery separate from hosting, API usage, and third-party costs — so you know exactly what you're paying for before we start.

If you've got an idea or a process that's eating up your time, just send me the problem, who it's for, and how you'd like it to work. I'll happily suggest the best way to build it.
